# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Faculty members, prospective advisors, research collaborators, and students who want a concise overview of Zeju Li's research profile, publications, and curriculum vitae.

## Product Purpose

Present Zeju Li's academic identity and current robotics research clearly, helping visitors understand his interests, contributions, and trajectory with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Academic, focused, and approachable. The page should feel personal and credible without becoming formal, commercial, or visually theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid commercial landing-page patterns, excessive decoration, conspicuous animation, dense navigation, and visual effects that compete with the research content. Preserve the restrained academic-portfolio character inspired by Jon Barron's website.

## Design Principles

1. Put research content and evidence before decoration.
2. Make the first screen easy to scan for identity, interests, and key links.
3. Use consistent alignment and spacing to create a calm reading rhythm.
4. Keep interaction lightweight and familiar across desktop and mobile.
5. Preserve a personal voice while maintaining academic credibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain clear text contrast, meaningful alternative text, keyboard-accessible links, and a responsive layout that remains readable on narrow screens. Avoid unnecessary motion and respect reduced-motion preferences when motion is introduced.
